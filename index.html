      How to play

      Find the hidden word in 8 guesses. Every guess is scored by meaning, not spelling — and the beach reacts to how close you are.

        Each day has a small clue about where the word lives. Stuck? Take a hint (it costs points, never a guess).

        Words the game doesn't know yet aren't counted. Plurals count as the same word.

        Scoring

        1000 for finding the word
        + 150 per unused guess
        + up to 300 for speed (−1 per 2 s)
        − 150 per hint
        Best possible: 2,350.

        The word engine

        AFTERWORD scores guesses with a hand-built semantic graph of about 5,000 everyday English words, written for this game.

        Each word sits in a category tree (e.g. animal → mammal → canine) and is linked to related words with a strength. Closeness combines the direct link, the best two-step link through a shared neighbour, and how specific the shared category is. Spelling plays no part.

        It's small and honest: if it doesn't know a word, it says so instead of guessing. The closeness number is a similarity estimate — not a probability.

        Daily answers are bundled in the page, so a determined player could find them in the source. Please don't spoil it for yourself!
